Restart Fusion and open Utilities > Scripts and Add-Ins.
Select HelixPathPilot under Add-Ins and click Run.
Optionally enable Run on Startup.

The command is available under Solid > Create.
Check the version displayed in the dialog after updating.
If the add-in is not listed, add the installed HelixPathPilot folder manually
through Fusion's Scripts and Add-Ins dialog.

To uninstall: close Fusion and use Windows Settings > Apps > HelixPathPilot.
Personal presets and files not installed by this setup are preserved.
